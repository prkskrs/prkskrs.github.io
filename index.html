Bio

Prakash Kumar Singh is an agentic engineer. He builds LLM agents, memory layers and the evaluation systems that keep them honest once they are in production. He is the founder of YourLio AI, was a founding engineer at AnswersAi before Quizlet acquired it, and contributes to the open source agent framework Agno. He teaches what he learns: his articles and developer videos have been read and watched more than 500K+ times.

Writing and teaching

  • Articles. 20+ pieces on Medium and dev.to about backends, data and agents, read 50K+ times.

  • Community. Ran a coding and mentorship community for engineers early in their careers, which is where most of the writing started.
